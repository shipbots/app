## Detailed description  (max 16,000 chars)

```
ShipBots Customer Service is the companion popup for the ShipBots Onboarding Dashboard. It puts the parts of a client record you look up 30 times a day one keystroke away — without having to open a new tab or switch context.

What you can do from the popup:
• Live search across every client on the shared Monday board. Matches name, email, phone (digits only), or portal login.
• Inline read-only info panel: contacts (up to three), billing address, receiving details, packing & shipping, returns, portal logins, warehouses, and the assigned support agent.
• See the client's shared Sticky Notes right alongside the info panel so any pinned annotations show up before you even ask.
• Quick launch of Mini Apps — the CSV Order Formatter is the first one; more join over time.
• Deep-link into the full dashboard for anything you need to edit; the popup itself stays read-only so it's fast and safe.

How it works:
The extension reuses your existing Google sign-in for the ShipBots dashboard — no separate account, no separate password. It talks only to the ShipBots dashboard backend, which is what serves the Monday-sourced client data.

Who this is for:
Members of the ShipBots customer-service and onboarding teams. This extension is only useful to people who already have access to the ShipBots dashboard.
```
